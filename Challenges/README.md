### `in-the-classroom` Module and Grader

The `in-the-classroom` materials include a grader designed for participants of this year's Qiskit Fall Fest.

The grader allows you to check your answers as you work through the notebooks. To activate it, simply run the **setup cell at the top of each notebook**.

> **Requirement:** You must have an active and valid **IBM Quantum Platform account** to use the grader.

## How to Participate

1. **Choose your Fall Fest project.**
   Decide what you would like to work on for your Qiskit Fall Fest project.

   > **Note:** Committing to complete one or more of the challenge materials provided above is sufficient as a project choice.

2. **Form a team of two.**

   * Each project team must consist of **two members**.
   * Members of the same team may submit the **same project plan**.
   * However, **each team member must send the project plan separately**.

3. **Fork this repository.**

   * Create your own **fork** of this repository.
   * Complete all project work in your forked repository.
   * Add your team member as a collaborator where necessary.

4. **Submit your project plan by 14 October 2026.**
   Send your project plan to `club.uiqc@gmail.com`.

   Your project plan should include:

   * Team name
   * Names of both team members
   * Project title
   * Brief description of the proposed project/challenge
   * Forked repository address

5. **Complete your project and prepare your final submission.**

   Your final submission must not exceed **6 pages**, structured as follows:

   * **1 page:** Project plan and team details
   * **Maximum of 4 pages:** Project presentation, methodology, results, and discussion
   * **1 page:** Declaration of all assistance and resources used, including **AI tools, references, external materials, mentorship, and how each was used**

6. **Submit your final project by 21 October 2026.**

   * Send your final submission by email to `club.uiqc@gmail.com`.
   * Use the following naming format:

     `teamname_uiqc-qff-project`

7. **Register your completed project in the main repository.**
   After submitting your project, add your **Team Name** and **Project Title** to the `Project.md` file in the main repository.

   This must be completed **on or before 11:59 PM WAT on 21 October 2026**.

## AI-Assisted Learning and Guided Mentorship

The challenge encourages the responsible use of **AI-assisted learning, documentation, tutorials, reference materials, and mentor guidance**.

AI tools may be used to:

* Explain unfamiliar quantum computing concepts
* Clarify Qiskit syntax and documentation
* Help debug code
* Explore alternative approaches to a problem
* Improve understanding of mathematical or physical concepts
* Support technical writing and presentation

However, AI should function as a **learning assistant rather than a substitute for understanding**. Participants should be able to explain the concepts, methods, code, and results presented in their final project.

All significant assistance received must be disclosed in the **Assistance Declaration** section of the final submission, including what tool or resource was used and how it contributed to the project.

## Most Importantly

Explore. Ask questions. Experiment. Break things. Debug them. Learn from your teammate, mentors, documentation, and AI-assisted tools.

The objective is not simply to produce working Qiskit code, but to develop a better understanding of **quantum computing through hands-on problem solving**.
